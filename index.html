About

I’m a software engineer with a strong interest in building things that are clear, useful, and well-crafted. I care about both how systems work and how they feel to use — the structure behind the experience, and the details that make something feel polished instead of merely functional.

Outside of software, I’m drawn to interests that reward strategy, precision, and steady improvement. A lot of them look different on the surface, but they share the same core appeal: depth, refinement, and the satisfaction of getting better at something that can’t be rushed.

This site is a curated home for some of that work and a few of those interests. It is meant to be a simple, personal hub rather than a full public life feed.

Interests

Outside of software, I spend time on a mix of competitive, strategic, and performance-oriented interests. These include gaming, pool, chess, fitness, and other pursuits that reward focus, adaptation, and repetition over time.
